Tutorial

The setup walkthrough opens on first launch and lives here afterwards — it walks through downloading a model, adding a recording and running it.

Where everything lives

Portable layout — everything lives beside the app, delete the folder and it’s gone. Whisper models land in models/. Each finished job gets its own results/<name>/ folder (.txt transcript, .json timestamped segments, .raw.md timestamped transcript or the LLM document, and .raw.txt when the glossary corrected words). Uploads and temp chunks live in data/work/.

Output types

Quitting

Close the app window to stop everything. If the app fell back to a browser tab, closing the tab also stops it (there is a short grace period for reloads), or use the Quit button in the left rail.

Troubleshooting

The app keeps a debug log of what the backend did: startup, chosen devices, every job and the full error text. If something misbehaves, open the folder and send minutenest.log.
